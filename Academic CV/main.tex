% ==================== TEACHING PROFILE ====================
\cvsection{\faUser}{Teaching Profile}

Educator and doctoral researcher with over seven years of undergraduate-level mathematics
tutoring and instruction in the City University of New York (CUNY) system, primarily as a
mathematics tutor at Borough of Manhattan Community College (BMCC). Additional teaching
experience includes supplemental instruction and Maple-based Calculus I laboratory instruction.
Experienced across the full undergraduate mathematics
curriculum, from developmental arithmetic and mathematical literacy through Calculus III,
Differential Equations, and Discrete Mathematics, with particular strength in gateway and
college-level courses serving diverse learner populations. Holds a Master of Science in Data
Analytics (GPA\,4.0) and a Bachelor of Science in Mathematics, providing subject-matter depth
for instruction in statistics, quantitative reasoning, and data analytics. Committed to
accessible, student-centered instruction in fully online and hybrid academic environments.
